Explanation

Converts markdown like this:

> "I am in fact a Hobbit in all but size." > > -- J.R.R. Tolkien

## Blog posts

- Why Glorfindel is More Impressive than Legolas - Why Tom Bombadil Was a Mistake - The Unparalleled Majesty of "The Lord of the Rings"

To HTML:

<blockquote>"I am in fact a Hobbit in all but size."  -- J.R.R. Tolkien</blockquote><h2>Blog posts</h2><ul><li><a href="/static-site-generator/static-site-generator/blog/glorfindel">Why Glorfindel is More Impressive than Legolas</a></li><li><a href="/static-site-generator/static-site-generator/blog/tom">Why Tom Bombadil Was a Mistake</a></li><li><a href="/static-site-generator/static-site-generator/blog/majesty">The Unparalleled Majesty of "The Lord of the Rings"</a></li></ul>

(It's not formatted prettily but it gets the job done.)

I built this project following this course on boot.dev. All of the pages on my site have been generated by this generator using markdown I wrote as input.
